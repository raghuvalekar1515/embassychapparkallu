The Apartment Clubhouse at Embassy South Reserve

The Apartment Clubhouse serves the 855 Eco Apartments. It is built around a mature anchor tree, wrapped in timber screens and greenery, and sits beside a pond and native gardens.

Its floor area, the facilities inside it and the date it opens will be confirmed with the project details and the agreement. When you visit, ask which of the community’s pools, fitness centres and rooms sit in this clubhouse.

With 855 Eco Apartments at this address, also ask how the busiest spaces, such as the courts and the banquet hall, will be booked.

The Villa Clubhouse at Embassy Riverine

The Villa Clubhouse serves the 217 Villa Homes. It is built into the landscape, with a long pool in front of its timber pavilion, and sits among the six landscaped corridors of Embassy Riverine.

Ask whether villa owners can also use the Apartment Clubhouse and the Rooftop Ribbon, and the reverse, and whether membership is part of the price or a separate line on the cost sheet.

Sports and fitness at Embassy Chapparkallu

Racquet sports are well covered, with courts for tennis, pickleball and padel and a multipurpose court. Swimming pools and fitness centres are planned, and for quieter exercise there are meditation and yoga rooms and a yoga deck.

The amenity list names swimming pools and fitness centres in the plural. Ask which address each one is at, and whether any are open to residents of both. Ask too whether the courts will be lit for evening play and whether coaching can be arranged.

The Rooftop Ribbon and its terraces

The Rooftop Ribbon is a walkway at the top of Embassy South Reserve that links all nine towers, edged by golden railings. Alongside it are terrace amenities for the apartment residents.

Because it links all nine towers, the Ribbon is also a route between neighbours in different towers, above the gardens. Ask which terrace amenities are planned along it and how residents of each tower reach it.

Spaces for children, seniors and pets

A kids’ zone and a children’s park are planned for younger residents, with a senior citizens’ zone for older ones and a pet park. Ask where the kids’ zone sits in relation to the towers and villas, so you can judge how far younger children would walk on their own.

For gatherings there are dining and banquet spaces and celebration and picnic lawns, useful for birthdays and family events without leaving the community.

The landscape as everyday space

At Embassy Origins much of the open space is landscape rather than buildings. The riparian corridor of about 41 acres links five lakes, about 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species are planted through the community, and the planting is chosen so that something flowers in every season.

Native trees raised in a nursery on the site and a landscape that flowers each season also mean the community will look different through the year, and will change as the trees grow. On a visit, ask which parts of the corridor will be open to residents and which will be kept for water and planting.

Embassy Chapparkallu amenities FAQs

How many clubhouses does Embassy Chapparkallu have?

Two, one for each address: the Apartment Clubhouse at Embassy South Reserve and the Villa Clubhouse at Embassy Riverine.

Which sports can be played at Embassy Chapparkallu?

Tennis, pickleball and padel on their own courts, with a multipurpose court, swimming pools and fitness centres.

What is the Rooftop Ribbon at Embassy Chapparkallu?

A walkway at the top of Embassy South Reserve that links all nine towers, with terrace amenities alongside.

Is there a pet park at Embassy Chapparkallu?

Yes. A pet park is planned, along with a kids’ zone, a children’s park and a senior citizens’ zone.

Are clubhouse charges included in the Embassy Chapparkallu price?

Ask for the cost sheet of the home you choose; it shows whether clubhouse charges apply and how much they are.
