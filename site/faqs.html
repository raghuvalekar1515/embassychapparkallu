Embassy Chapparkallu FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about “Embassy Chapparkallu” project.

About the project

What is Embassy Chapparkallu?

Embassy Chapparkallu is Embassy Origins, an 85.5-acre community by Embassy near Tarahunise Lake in North Bengaluru. Its first phase brings 217 Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine and 855 Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve, set around a riparian corridor of about 41 acres that links five lakes.

Are Embassy Chapparkallu and Embassy Origins the same project?

Yes. Embassy Origins is the name of the 85.5-acre community, and Embassy Chapparkallu is the name many buyers use for it, taken from Chapparkallu Road, which the land sits on near Tarahunise Lake. Embassy Riverine and Embassy South Reserve are not separate projects but the two addresses inside the first phase, one for the Villa Homes and one for the Eco Apartments. Your cost sheet and agreement will name the address of your home.

What are Embassy Riverine and Embassy South Reserve?

They are the two addresses of the first phase. Embassy Riverine is the villa address: 217 Villa Homes of 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK on about 55 acres, each facing one of six landscaped corridors. Embassy South Reserve is the apartment address: 855 Eco Apartments from a Studio to 3.5 BHK in nine towers set in an arc towards Tarahunise Lake. Each has its own clubhouse, and both share the lakes and the riparian corridor.

What makes the master plan special?

It follows the land’s own water and air: a central riparian corridor of about 41 acres links five lakes, about 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species form a green spine, and the landscape is planted so that something flowers in every season.

Who designed Embassy Origins?

Bhumiputra Architecture designed and landscaped the community, with architect Alok Shetty leading the team. The plan follows the golden ratio as well as the natural slope and water of the site, so the riparian corridor and its five lakes are the fixed points and the villas and towers are placed around them. For a buyer, that means a home’s outlook depends on where it sits relative to the corridor, which is worth checking on the layout plan.

When was Embassy Chapparkallu launched?

The first phase of Embassy Origins went on sale at its launch on 16 September 2026, and within its first week 477 homes had been booked. Because bookings continue, the homes still open in each tower or villa corridor keep changing. This website’s figures are as of 21 October 2026, so when you enquire, ask which homes of the type you want remain and the date the price you are quoted applies to.

Is Embassy Chapparkallu ready to move in?

No. It is a new launch, so homes are chosen from plans, pictures and the layout rather than walked through finished. A possession date for the first phase is not given on this website; it is set out in the agreement for sale and on the cost sheet of the home you book. Get it in writing, and ask how buyers will be kept informed as construction moves through its stages.

Will Embassy Origins have more phases?

The homes described on this website, 217 Villa Homes and 855 Eco Apartments, make up the first phase of the 85.5-acre community. Plans for any later phase, including what it would hold and when it would launch, are not set out here. If future construction near your home matters to you, ask the sales team which parts of the land the first phase covers and what is planned around it.

Homes, sizes and plans

What homes are available?

Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve in Studio, 2, 2.5, 3 and 3.5 BHK layouts of about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft, and Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine in 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK of about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft.

What sizes are the Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve?

The Eco Apartments come as a Studio and as 2, 2.5, 3 and 3.5 BHK layouts, and together they span about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft. That span covers the whole collection, so it does not give the area of any single type. The area of each type, and of the exact home where it varies, comes with its floor plan, which the sales team shares on request.

How large are the Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine?

The 217 Villa Homes are 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK, and the collection spans about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft. The main living, dining and kitchen spaces open straight onto the garden, and every villa faces one of six landscaped corridors: flowering, fragrant, open grass, edible, active or shaded. Ask for the built-up area and the garden or land area as separate figures, since both shape what you are paying for.

What does a half BHK, such as 2.5 or 4.5 BHK, mean?

A half usually signals one extra room that is smaller than a full bedroom, often used as a study, a home office or a guest room. At Embassy Chapparkallu the half appears in the 2.5 and 3.5 BHK Eco Apartments and in the 4.5 BHK Villa Home. The floor plan of each shows what that room is, how large it is and whether it has a bathroom of its own.

Are the sizes quoted as carpet area or super built-up area?

This website gives approximate sizes without naming the measure behind them. Carpet area is the usable floor inside a home’s walls; super built-up area also counts the walls, balconies and a portion of shared spaces such as lobbies and lift wells, so it is always the bigger number. When you compare two homes here, or a home here with one elsewhere, make sure both figures use the same measure.

How do the towers affect the choice of an Eco Apartment?

The nine towers of Embassy South Reserve form an arc that descends from east to west towards Tarahunise Lake, so their heights and outlooks differ. When you shortlist an Eco Apartment, ask which tower it is in, which floor, whether the living room faces the lake or the gardens, and how far it is from the Apartment Clubhouse and from the way up to the Rooftop Ribbon.

How do I get the floor plan of a specific home?

Use the enquiry form and name the address and type you want, for example a 3 BHK Eco Apartment at Embassy South Reserve or a 4.5 BHK Villa Home at Embassy Riverine. The sales team sends the plans available for that type with the latest price list, or the latest project details if the brochure is not out yet. The Floor Plans page lists every type by address.

Price, payment and home loans

What is the price of Embassy Chapparkallu?

Prices are shared on request. Enquire to receive the latest price list and availability for the Villa Home or Eco Apartment you prefer.

Why are Embassy Chapparkallu prices given only on request?

Prices are quoted for each home rather than as one headline figure. The homes run from Eco Apartments of about 1,042 sq.ft to Villa Homes of about 7,388 sq.ft, and two homes of one type may be priced differently for their tower, their place in the arc or their villa corridor. Availability has also moved quickly since the launch, so a dated quote for the exact home you want is the figure worth comparing.

What should I compare on two cost sheets?

Compare them line by line: the area each price is based on and the measure used, the base price, car parking, any charge for the Apartment Clubhouse or the Villa Clubhouse, maintenance collected before handover, taxes, and the estimate for stamp duty and registration. A charge missing from one sheet may be excluded or simply not yet listed, so ask which. Keep every cost sheet with the date it was issued.

What costs are paid on top of the home’s price?

Stamp duty and registration are extra, paid on top of the price of the home. Other charges, such as car parking, clubhouse membership, maintenance deposits and taxes, may appear as separate lines on the cost sheet rather than inside the base price. Ask for a cost sheet that shows the full amount for the exact home, so that you compare totals and not base prices alone.

How does the payment plan at Embassy Chapparkallu work?

Payment plans come with the price list, matched to the home you choose. At a new launch they are usually construction-linked: you pay a booking amount, then instalments as building work reaches stages named in the plan, then the balance at handover. Check how many stages there are, what share of the price falls due at each, and how many days you have to pay once a demand reaches you.

Can I buy a home at Embassy Chapparkallu with a home loan?

Yes, a home loan can fund part of the price, subject to the lender’s own review of you and of the project. The booking amount usually comes from your savings, and on a construction-linked plan the bank pays its share in parts as each demand is raised. Ask lenders early whether they will fund a home at Embassy Origins, how much they would lend for the home you want, and whether interest-only payments apply until the loan is fully paid out.

What should I check before paying a booking amount?

First, that the home is named exactly: address, type, tower or villa corridor, and area. Second, that you hold a dated cost sheet and the payment plan for it. Third, the refund terms if you change your mind. Finally, that the seller named on the receipt is the company in the agreement and the money goes to its own account. This website registers interest only and never collects money, so do not pay anyone on its behalf.

Location and connectivity

Where is Embassy Chapparkallu located?

On Chapparkallu Road near Tarahunise Lake in North Yelahanka, off NH44 (Bellary Road) on the approach to Kempegowda International Airport, about 20 minutes away. Stonehill International School is about 2.2 km away and Rajanukunte railway station about 6.9 km.

How long is the drive to Kempegowda International Airport?

The airport is about 15.5 km away by road, about 20 minutes along NH44 (Bellary Road), the highway the site sits just off. That short run is one of the main reasons to look at Embassy Chapparkallu if you fly often or work around the airport. Travel times change with traffic, so try the route at the hours you would usually travel before deciding.

Which railway stations are closest to Embassy Chapparkallu?

Rajanukunte Railway Station is the nearest, about 6.9 km from the site. Yelahanka Junction Railway Station, to the south, is about 13.4 km away. Both are approximate driving distances. If you plan to rely on rail for work or travel, check the trains and timings that suit your route, and how you would get from the station back to your door.

Which schools are near Embassy Chapparkallu?

Stonehill International School is the closest school listed, about 2.2 km away, which keeps the daily drop short. Canadian International School is about 13.5 km away, and Yelahanka, about 13 km to the south, has more schools. These are approximate driving distances; check admissions, fees and bus routes directly with each school before you choose a home on that basis.

Where are the nearest hospital and shopping mall?

The nearest listed mall is The Galleria Mall in Yelahanka, about 13.7 km away, and the nearest listed hospital is Aster CMI Hospital in Hebbal, about 20.4 km away. Shops, pharmacies and clinics closer to the site are not listed on this website, so on your visit note how far you would drive for groceries, medicines and emergency care. Many larger errands will mean a drive south towards Yelahanka.

Is Embassy Chapparkallu practical for a daily commute into the city?

It depends on where you work. Manyata Tech Park is about 22.2 km by road and Aster CMI Hospital in Hebbal about 20.4 km, so a daily drive into the city is a long one. The location makes more sense if your office is close to the airport or north of the city, if you travel a lot, or if you work from home part of the week. Drive your own route at peak hours before you commit.

Amenities and daily life

What amenities are planned?

An Apartment Clubhouse built around a mature anchor tree and a Villa Clubhouse built into the landscape, with swimming pools, fitness centres, yoga and meditation rooms, dining and banquet halls, tennis, pickleball and padel courts, kids’ zones, a pet park, a senior citizens’ zone, lawns and the Rooftop Ribbon.

Which clubhouse will I use at Embassy Chapparkallu?

Each address has its own. Apartment owners at Embassy South Reserve are served by the Apartment Clubhouse, built around a mature anchor tree and set beside a pond, in timber screens and greenery. Villa owners at Embassy Riverine are served by the Villa Clubhouse, a timber pavilion with a long pool in front, set into the landscape. Ask whether owners at one address may use the other clubhouse, and whether membership is priced separately.

What is the Rooftop Ribbon?

The Rooftop Ribbon is a walkway running across the tops of all nine towers at Embassy South Reserve, edged by golden railings, with terrace amenities alongside it. It gives apartment residents a route between towers above the gardens. Ask which terrace amenities line it, how residents of each tower get up to it, and whether villa owners at Embassy Riverine will have access too.

What sports and fitness facilities are planned?

Courts for tennis, pickleball and padel, a multipurpose court, swimming pools and fitness centres, with meditation and yoga rooms and a yoga deck for gentler exercise. Pools and fitness centres are listed in the plural, so ask which of them sit at your address, and how the busiest facilities will be shared between 855 apartment homes and 217 villas once both addresses are lived in.

What is there for children, older residents and pets?

Younger children have a kids’ zone and a children’s park, older residents a senior citizens’ zone, and pet owners a pet park. For family occasions there are dining and banquet spaces and lawns set aside for celebrations and picnics. When you visit, check where the kids’ zone sits in relation to your tower or villa corridor, so you can judge whether children could walk there on their own.

How green will Embassy Origins be?

The plan gives a large share of the land to landscape: about 41 acres of riparian corridor joining five lakes, and roughly 4,000 native trees, more than 100 species in all, grown in an on-site nursery and forming a green spine. Planting is chosen so the community has something in flower in every season. Young trees take years to fill out, so ask how large they will be at handover near your home.

The developer: Embassy

Who is the developer?

Embassy Developments, part of Embassy Group, a privately held Bengaluru group chaired by Jitendra (Jitu) Virwani that began operations in 1993. Its Bengaluru homes include Embassy Lake Terraces in Hebbal and the Embassy Springs township in North Bengaluru.

Which company sells the homes at Embassy Chapparkallu?

The developer named for the project is Embassy Developments, part of Embassy Group. The group’s residential business is now largely run through Embassy Developments Limited, formerly Equinox India Developments Limited, following the merger with Indiabulls Real Estate that was completed in 2025. Read the agreement for sale to see which company sells your home, and check that every receipt carries that same name.

Is Embassy Group the same as Embassy Office Parks REIT?

No. Three names are often mixed up. Embassy Group is the privately held group, based in Bengaluru, behind the Embassy brand. Embassy Developments Limited is its listed residential company, traded on BSE under scrip code 532832 and on NSE under the symbol EMBDL. Embassy Office Parks REIT, listed in 2019, is a separate listed entity and is not the company that sells homes at Embassy Chapparkallu.

Where is Embassy based, and who leads it?

Embassy Group is a Bengaluru group that began operations in 1993 and is led by its chairman, Jitendra (Jitu) Virwani. Its corporate office is on the 14th floor of Pinnacle Tower at Hebbal Mill on Bellary Road, on the same side of the city as Embassy Chapparkallu, and its registered office is at Embassy Point on Infantry Road. The group works in commercial, residential, hospitality, services and education spaces.

Has Embassy completed homes near Embassy Chapparkallu?

Yes. On the same side of the city, Embassy Lake Terraces in Hebbal, with condominiums and sky villas, is completed, and so is Embassy Boulevard, a villa community off Bellary Road towards the airport. The Embassy Springs township near the airport on NH44 is ongoing. Walking through a completed Embassy community shows how finishes, common areas and landscaping hold up after handover, which a new launch cannot yet show.

What other homes has Embassy built in Bengaluru?

Beyond the north of the city, Embassy’s homes include Embassy Pristine in Bellandur, off the Outer Ring Road, 424 apartments on 14.5 acres and now completed; Embassy Grove off Old Airport Road, 106 villaments on 7 acres, ready to move in; and Embassy East Avenue in Whitefield, apartments launched in 2024 and still ongoing. Embassy Chapparkallu brings villas and apartments together in a single community.
