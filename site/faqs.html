Embassy Chapparkallu FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about “Embassy Chapparkallu” project.

What is Embassy Chapparkallu?

Embassy Chapparkallu is Embassy Origins, an 85.5-acre community by Embassy near Tarahunise Lake in North Bengaluru. Its first phase brings 217 Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine and 855 Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve, set around a riparian corridor of about 41 acres that links five lakes.

Where is Embassy Chapparkallu located?

On Chapparkallu Road near Tarahunise Lake in North Yelahanka, off NH44 (Bellary Road) on the approach to Kempegowda International Airport, about 20 minutes away. Stonehill International School is about 2.2 km away and Rajanukunte railway station about 6.9 km.

What homes are available?

Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve in Studio, 2, 2.5, 3 and 3.5 BHK layouts of about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft, and Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine in 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK of about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft.

What is the price of Embassy Chapparkallu?

Prices are shared on request. Enquire to receive the latest price list and availability for the Villa Home or Eco Apartment you prefer.

What makes the master plan special?

It follows the land’s own water and air: a central riparian corridor of about 41 acres links five lakes, about 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species form a green spine, and the landscape is planted so that something flowers in every season.

What amenities are planned?

An Apartment Clubhouse built around a mature anchor tree and a Villa Clubhouse built into the landscape, with swimming pools, fitness centres, yoga and meditation rooms, dining and banquet halls, tennis, pickleball and padel courts, kids’ zones, a pet park, a senior citizens’ zone, lawns and the Rooftop Ribbon.

Who is the developer?

Embassy, with a legacy of more than 30 years, a portfolio of 38 million sq.ft and over 40 completed projects. Its Bengaluru homes include Embassy Lake Terraces in Hebbal and the Embassy Springs township in North Bengaluru.
