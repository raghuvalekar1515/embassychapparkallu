Airport and rail links from Embassy Chapparkallu

The site is reached from Bellary Road (NH44). Kempegowda International Airport is about 15.5 km away, about 20 minutes by road, which makes the project practical for anyone who flies often or works near the airport.

For rail, Rajanukunte Railway Station is the closest, at about 6.9 km. Yelahanka Junction Railway Station is about 13.4 km away, towards Yelahanka.

If you are flying in to see the project, the site is about 15.5 km from the airport by road, so a visit can be planned around a flight.

These are approximate driving distances. Travel times change through the day, so check each route at the hours you would actually travel.

Schools near Embassy Chapparkallu

Stonehill International School is about 2.2 km from the site, the closest school on the list here. Canadian International School is about 13.5 km away, and Yelahanka’s other schools are about 13 km to the south.

For families, the short distance to Stonehill matters for the daily school run. For any other school, drive the route at school time before you decide, and check admissions directly with the school.

Hospitals, shopping and daily needs

The Galleria Mall in Yelahanka is about 13.7 km away, and Yelahanka’s malls are about 13 km to the south. Aster CMI Hospital in Hebbal is about 20.4 km away.

Shops and clinics nearer the site are not listed on this website. Ask about them on your site visit, and note how far you would drive for groceries, a pharmacy and emergency care.

Yelahanka, about 13 km to the south, has its own schools, malls and railway junction, so expect many errands to mean a drive in that direction.

Commuting to work from Embassy Chapparkallu

Manyata Tech Park is about 22.2 km away, and Hebbal about 20.4 km, measured to Aster CMI Hospital there.

That makes Embassy Chapparkallu a better fit for people who work near the airport or on the northern edge of the city, travel often, or work from home part of the week, than for someone who drives into the city every day. If your office is south of Hebbal, drive the route at peak hours before you choose.

Hybrid work changes the sums: a long drive on some days of the week weighs differently from the same drive every day.

Tarahunise Lake and the setting

The site takes its character from water. It is near Tarahunise Lake, the nine towers of Embassy South Reserve step down from east to west towards the lake, and a riparian corridor of about 41 acres links five lakes through the community.

On a site visit, look at the land towards the lake, see where the towers and the villa corridors sit in relation to it, and ask which homes look over water and which over gardens.

Note the approach as well: the roads from NH44 to the site, and how long the last stretch takes at different times of day.
