Embassy Chapparkallu Location

Embassy Chapparkallu is on Chapparkallu Road near Tarahunise Lake in North Yelahanka, off NH44 (Bellary Road) on the approach to Kempegowda International Airport, about 20 minutes away. Stonehill International School is about 2.2 km away and Rajanukunte railway station about 6.9 km, with Yelahanka’s schools, malls and railway junction about 13 km to the south.
