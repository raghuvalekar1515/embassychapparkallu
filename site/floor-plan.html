Reading the Embassy Chapparkallu size ranges

Each range on this page covers a whole collection, not a single home type. The lower figure is the smallest home in that collection and the upper figure the largest, so the range alone does not tell you the area of, say, a 2.5 BHK or a 4.5 BHK.

When you ask for floor plans, ask for the area of the exact type and, where it varies, of the exact home. Sizes here are approximate; the figure on the cost sheet and in the agreement is the one that counts.

A half in a configuration, as in 2.5, 3.5 or 4.5 BHK, usually means one smaller extra room, often used as a study or a home office. The floor plan shows what that room is in each type here.

Carpet area and super built-up area

Home sizes are quoted in two common ways. Carpet area is the floor area inside the home’s walls that you can actually use. Super built-up area adds the walls, balconies and a share of the common areas, such as lobbies, corridors and lift wells, so it is always the larger figure for the same home.

The sizes on this site, about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft for the Eco Apartments and about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft for the Villa Homes, are approximate and do not state which measure they use. Ask which measure the floor plan and the cost sheet use, and compare homes only on the same one.

For a Villa Home, ask for the built-up area of the villa and the area of any land or garden that comes with it as separate figures, since the garden matters as much as the rooms.

Inside an Eco Apartment at Embassy South Reserve

The Eco Apartments sit in nine towers that rise in a flowing arc and step down from east to west towards Tarahunise Lake. A sample living room shows a garden view, and the towers are joined at the top by the Rooftop Ribbon, a walkway that links all nine.

When you read an Eco Apartment floor plan, look at:

  • which tower the home is in, since the towers step down towards the lake;
  • which way the living room faces, towards the lake or the gardens;
  • how the bedrooms and the kitchen are arranged around the living area;
  • how close the home is to the Apartment Clubhouse, built around a mature anchor tree.

Inside a Villa Home at Embassy Riverine

Each of the 217 Villa Homes sits on one of six landscaped corridors across about 55 acres, with a riparian water corridor running past. Inside, the living, dining and kitchen open onto the garden, so the plan is built around the link between the main rooms and the outdoors.

When you read a Villa Home floor plan, check:

  • which corridor the villa faces: flowering, fragrant, open grass, edible, active or shaded;
  • how the garden connects to the living, dining and kitchen;
  • the number of levels and where the bedrooms sit, which the plan shows;
  • the walk to the Villa Clubhouse, built into the landscape.

Choosing between the Embassy Chapparkallu home types

A Studio or 2 BHK Eco Apartment suits a single buyer or a couple who want a compact home on the airport side of the city. A 2.5 or 3 BHK gives a growing family an extra room. The 3.5 BHK, the top Eco Apartment type, suits a family that wants apartment living with space for a study or a guest.

The Villa Homes start where the apartments stop. A 4, 4.5 or 5 BHK villa of about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft suits a large or multi-generation household, or a buyer who wants a garden that the main rooms open onto.
