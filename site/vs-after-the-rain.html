Embassy Chapparkallu vs After the Rain

Embassy Chapparkallu and Total Environment After the Rain are both villa communities on North Bengaluru’s airport corridor, in the Yelahanka area off Bellary Road, and both are planned around landscape. They differ in size, in stage and in what they sell. This guide sets them side by side.

Location: Tarahunise and Hosahalli

Embassy Chapparkallu is on Chapparkallu Road near Tarahunise Lake in North Yelahanka, off NH44. Kempegowda International Airport is about 15.5 km away by road, about 20 minutes, and Stonehill International School about 2.2 km.

After the Rain is on Sir MVIT College Road in Hosahalli, Yelahanka, about 2.8 km from Bellary Road. Kempegowda International Airport and Manyata Tech Park are each about 12 km away, and the upcoming Namma Metro Blue Line to the airport runs about 3.6 km from the community.

The two sets of distances are measured differently: driving distances for Embassy Chapparkallu, straight-line distances for After the Rain. A straight-line figure is never longer than the drive, so time both routes yourself before you compare commutes.

The villas compared

Embassy Chapparkallu’s 217 Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine come in 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK, from about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft. Each sits on one of six landscaped corridors, flowering, fragrant, open grass, edible, active or shaded, and its living, dining and kitchen open onto the garden.

After the Rain’s 245 villas are all 4 BHK duplexes, earth-sheltered under green roofs, with courtyards, gardens and decks, built in exposed terracotta brick, natural wood and stone. The V45 villa has 4,824 sq.ft on a 4,608 sq.ft plot, and the V50 villa 5,120 to 5,547 sq.ft on a 4,521 sq.ft plot.

The choice is between range and a single, defined home. Embassy Riverine spans 4 to 5 BHK across a wider size band, while After the Rain offers one 4 BHK form in two sizes, with the plot area stated.

Apartments: only at Embassy Chapparkallu

After the Rain is a villa-only community. Embassy Chapparkallu also has 855 Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve, from a Studio to 3.5 BHK and about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft, in nine towers stepping down towards Tarahunise Lake and linked at the top by the Rooftop Ribbon.

If your budget or household points to an apartment rather than a villa, only one of these two projects belongs on your list.

Land, landscape and amenities

Embassy Chapparkallu sits on about 85.5 acres, around a riparian corridor of about 41 acres that links five lakes, with about 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species. Each address has its own clubhouse, and the amenities include swimming pools, fitness centres, courts for tennis, pickleball and padel, a pet park and a senior citizens’ zone.

After the Rain is laid out on 45 acres as rows of duplex villas linked by quiet internal driveways and set among landscaped green spaces. Its clubhouse has a swimming pool, a heated pool and a toddlers’ pool, a gymnasium, squash courts, billiards, table tennis, a library and a multipurpose hall, with a children’s play area.

Stage and timing

Timing is a key practical difference. Embassy Chapparkallu is a new launch: its first phase was launched in September 2026, prices are on request, and the possession timeline is confirmed in the agreement for the home you choose.

After the Rain’s possession runs phase-wise from December 2020 onwards, and its first homes are already handed over to their owners. There you can walk through finished villas; at Embassy Chapparkallu you buy from plans, with a payment plan shared with the price list.

Who each one suits

Embassy Chapparkallu suits buyers who:

  • want to choose between a villa and an apartment, from a Studio to a 5 BHK;
  • want a villa of up to about 7,388 sq.ft;
  • are comfortable buying at launch and waiting for construction;
  • value a large site with lakes, a riparian corridor and a clubhouse for each address;
  • want Stonehill International School about 2.2 km away.

After the Rain suits buyers who:

  • want a 4 BHK villa in a community where homes are already handed over;
  • like an earth-sheltered, green-roofed design with courtyards and decks;
  • want a stated plot area with the villa;
  • want to be about 2.8 km from Bellary Road, with the upcoming Namma Metro Blue Line about 3.6 km away.

Embassy Chapparkallu vs After the Rain in summary

Both projects offer large villas near Bellary Road in the Yelahanka area. Embassy Chapparkallu is the larger and newer community, with villas and apartments on about 85.5 acres and prices on request. After the Rain is a villa-only community of 245 homes on 45 acres, already in phase-wise possession. Embassy Chapparkallu vs After the Rain FAQs

Which is larger, Embassy Chapparkallu or After the Rain?

Embassy Chapparkallu, on about 85.5 acres, against 45 acres for After the Rain.

Does After the Rain have apartments?

No. After the Rain has 245 villas, all 4 BHK. Embassy Chapparkallu has both Villa Homes and Eco Apartments.

Which project is further along?

After the Rain, with possession phase-wise from December 2020 onwards and its first homes handed over. Embassy Chapparkallu is a new launch from September 2026.

How do the villa sizes compare?

Embassy Chapparkallu’s Villa Homes are about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft. After the Rain’s V45 is 4,824 sq.ft and its V50 5,120 to 5,547 sq.ft.

Which is closer to the airport?

The figures are not measured the same way: Embassy Chapparkallu is about 15.5 km by road, After the Rain about 12 km in a straight line. Drive both routes to compare.
