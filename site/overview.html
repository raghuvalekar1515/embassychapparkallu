Embassy Chapparkallu Project Overview

Embassy Chapparkallu is Embassy Origins, an 85.5-acre community by Embassy near Tarahunise Lake in North Yelahanka, off NH44 (Bellary Road) on the approach to Kempegowda International Airport. Its first phase holds two addresses: Embassy Riverine, 217 Villa Homes of 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK (about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft) along six landscaped corridors and a riparian water corridor, and Embassy South Reserve, 855 Eco Apartments from Studio to 3.5 BHK (about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft) in nine towers that step down in a flowing arc towards the lake. A central riparian corridor of about 41 acres links five lakes, and about 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species, raised in an on-site nursery, form a green spine through the community.

Embassy Chapparkallu at a glance

Embassy Chapparkallu and Embassy Origins are two names for the same community. Embassy Chapparkallu is the name most buyers search for, after Chapparkallu Road, the road the site sits on near Tarahunise Lake. Embassy Origins is the name of the 85.5-acre community itself, and Embassy Riverine and Embassy South Reserve are the two addresses inside its first phase.

Put simply, this is one piece of land with two kinds of home on it: Villa Homes for buyers who want a large villa, and Eco Apartments, from a Studio up to 3.5 BHK, for those who want an apartment. Both share the same lakes, the same riparian corridor and the same native planting, while each address has a clubhouse of its own.

How the land shapes Embassy Origins

The plan of Embassy Origins starts from the land rather than from a grid of plots and towers. A riparian corridor of about 41 acres follows the natural slope of the site and links five lakes, and the homes are arranged around it. About 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species, raised in a nursery on the site, are planted as a green spine through the community.

Bhumiputra Architecture, led by architect Alok Shetty, designed and landscaped the community, and the layout is set out around the golden ratio. The Master Plan page explains how the corridors, the lakes and the towers fit together, and how to read the plan when you choose a home.

Embassy Origins was launched on 16 September 2026, and 477 homes were booked within its first week. The project is a new launch: prices are shared on request, and the homes still open for booking change as bookings come in.

Who Embassy Chapparkallu suits

  • Villa buyers who want room: the Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine are 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK of about 4,362 to 7,388 sq.ft, each on one of six landscaped corridors, with the living, dining and kitchen opening onto the garden.
  • Apartment buyers across a wide span of sizes: the Eco Apartments at Embassy South Reserve run from a Studio to 3.5 BHK, about 1,042 to 2,227 sq.ft, in nine towers stepping down towards Tarahunise Lake.
  • People who fly often: the site is off NH44 (Bellary Road), about 20 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.
  • Families who want Stonehill International School close by, at about 2.2 km.
  • Buyers who value open land: about 41 acres of riparian corridor, five lakes and about 4,000 native trees are part of the plan.

It suits buyers less well if they need a short daily drive into the city. Manyata Tech Park is about 22.2 km away and Aster CMI Hospital in Hebbal about 20.4 km.

Where Embassy Chapparkallu sits in North Bengaluru

The community lies in North Yelahanka, on the airport side of the city, off Bellary Road (NH44), the road that leads north to Kempegowda International Airport, about 15.5 km away, and south towards Yelahanka and Hebbal. Rajanukunte railway station is about 6.9 km from the site, and Yelahanka Junction about 13.4 km.
