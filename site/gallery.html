What the Embassy Chapparkallu pictures show

  • Towers over the lake. The towers of Embassy South Reserve, joined at the top by the Rooftop Ribbon. All nine step down from east to west towards Tarahunise Lake.
  • A villa street. A tree-lined street of Villa Homes at Embassy Riverine, where every villa sits on one of six landscaped corridors.
  • Apartment Clubhouse. Timber screens beside a pond and native gardens. The clubhouse is built around a mature anchor tree.
  • A pavilion at Embassy Riverine. A sweeping timber roof set into the landscape among the Villa Homes.
  • A tower entrance. A planted forecourt at one of the towers of Embassy South Reserve.
  • A sample living room. The living room of an Eco Apartment, with a view out to the garden.
  • Riparian water corridor. Water running past the Villa Homes, part of the corridor of about 41 acres that links five lakes.
  • Rooftop Ribbon. The walkway and its golden railings, linking all nine towers.
  • Villa Clubhouse. A long pool in front of its timber pavilion, built into the landscape.
