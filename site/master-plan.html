How to read the Embassy Chapparkallu master plan

A master plan is a map of the whole site from above. Read it in this order:

  • Find the boundary and the entrances, and how they connect to Chapparkallu Road.
  • Trace the riparian corridor and the five lakes it links. They are the fixed features everything else is arranged around.
  • Locate the two addresses: Embassy Riverine’s villas on their six corridors, and the arc of nine towers at Embassy South Reserve.
  • Mark the Apartment Clubhouse and the Villa Clubhouse, and see how far your likely home is from each.
  • Check the internal roads, parking and service areas, which the detailed plan shows.

The aerial picture on this page is an illustration, not a plan to scale. Ask for the layout plan with the exact home you are considering marked on it.

The riparian corridor and the five lakes

At the centre of the plan is a riparian corridor of about 41 acres. Riparian means land along a watercourse. Here the corridor follows the natural slope of the site and links five lakes, which is why the plan is described as following the land’s own water and air.

For a buyer, the corridor decides much of the outlook. Ask which homes border it and which look onto it from further away.

Embassy Riverine’s six landscape corridors

The 217 Villa Homes are arranged along six ecological corridors on about 55 acres, with a riparian water corridor running past. Each corridor has its own character: flowering, fragrant, open grass, edible, active and shaded.

Every villa sits on one of the six, so the corridor is part of the choice of home. A buyer who wants a quiet garden outlook may lean towards the shaded or fragrant corridors, while a family with children may prefer to be near the active one. Ask for the planting plan of the corridor you are considering.

Embassy South Reserve’s arc of nine towers

The nine towers of Embassy South Reserve rise in a flowing arc and step down from east to west towards Tarahunise Lake. The Rooftop Ribbon, a walkway, links all nine at the top, and the Apartment Clubhouse is built around a mature anchor tree.

Because the towers step down towards the lake, their heights differ across the arc. Ask for the floor count of each tower and where your home sits in the arc.

Trees, nursery and seasons at Embassy Origins

About 4,000 native trees of more than 100 species are part of the plan. They are raised in a nursery on the site and planted as a green spine through the community, and the landscape is planned so that something flowers in every season.

Native species are those that grow naturally in the region. Ask how mature the trees will be at handover and which of them line your part of the community.

The design team behind Embassy Origins

Embassy Origins was designed and landscaped by Bhumiputra Architecture, led by architect Alok Shetty. The layout is set out around the golden ratio and around the water and air of the site.

On the ground, that means a plan arranged around the corridor, the lakes and the planting, with the villas and towers placed along them rather than the other way round.
