Embassy Chapparkallu Master Plan

Embassy Origins is laid out around the golden ratio and around the land’s own water and air, designed and landscaped by Bhumiputra Architecture, led by architect Alok Shetty. A central riparian corridor of about 41 acres follows the natural slope and links five lakes. Embassy Riverine’s Villa Homes sit along six ecological corridors, flowering, fragrant, open grass, edible, active and shaded, while Embassy South Reserve’s nine towers rise in a flowing arc, stepping down from east to west towards Tarahunise Lake, joined at the top by the Rooftop Ribbon, a walkway that links all nine.
